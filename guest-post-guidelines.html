Editorial Standards

Guest Post Submission Requirements

To maintain high E-E-A-T authority and satisfy Google's Helpful Content criteria, every submission must strictly adhere to our editorial guidelines:

1. Content Quality & Originality

  • • 100% Unique & Original: Must pass Copyscape and originality audits. No spun or duplicated articles.
  • • Human-First Writing: We reject low-effort, raw AI-generated content. Posts must showcase real experience, data, or actionable takeaways.
  • • Word Count: Minimum 1,200 words (1,500 - 2,500 words strongly preferred for in-depth pillar pieces).

2. Accepted Niches & Topics

  • • Search Engine Optimization (Technical, On-Page, Off-Page, Local SEO)
  • • Guest Posting, Blogger Outreach & Link Building Strategies
  • • SaaS, Cloud Platforms, AI Tools & Web Development
  • • Digital Marketing, PPC, CRO, Social Media & Paid Media Funnels
  • • eCommerce, Direct-to-Consumer Growth & Business Strategy

3. Formatting & Link Policy

  • • Heading Hierarchy: Clear H1, H2, and H3 structure with short, readable paragraphs (2-3 sentences max).
  • • Outbound Links: Maximum 1-2 relevant contextual do-follow links to your non-competing website.
  • • Authoritative Citations: Include at least 2-3 links to high-authority references (e.g. Google Search Central, Moz, Ahrefs).
  • • Anchor Text: Must be natural and descriptive. No keyword stuffing or exact-match spam.

4. Strictly Prohibited Content

We strictly reject articles or links relating to gambling, casino, adult/pornographic content, illegal substances, payday loans, aggressive affiliate spam, or deceptive practices.

5. Editorial Review Timeline

Our editorial team reviews submissions within 24 to 48 hours. Once approved, you will receive a live URL confirmation and social syndication notice.
