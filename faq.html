📝 Guest Posting & Outreach FAQs

How do you ensure the websites are real and have genuine organic traffic? ▼
Every publisher domain in our database is verified using Ahrefs and Semrush. We look for continuous upward or stable organic keyword ranking graphs, active real visitor traffic, verified Moz Domain Authority (DA 30-90+), and a Moz Spam Score of less than 2%. We never use automated link farms or PBNs.
Do I get to write the articles, or does RankRise create them? ▼
All our guest post packages include 100% native, professional content authoring (1,200 to 2,000 words) written by our in-house subject matter copywriters. If you prefer to supply your own pre-written articles, we are happy to review and submit them according to each host site's editorial standards.
Can I choose my own anchor text and landing page URLs? ▼
Yes! You have full control over your target URLs and anchor text. If desired, our senior SEO team will also audit your current backlink profile and recommend optimal anchor text ratios to prevent over-optimization flags.

⚙️ Technical SEO & Retainer FAQs

How long does it take to see ranking improvements from SEO? ▼
While technical fixes (indexing, canonical tags, Core Web Vitals) often yield rapid indexation lifts within 2 to 4 weeks, competitive keyword ranking movements typically compound between months 2 and 4 as high-DA link equity permeates your topical clusters.
Are your SEO strategies safe from Google Core Algorithm updates? ▼
Yes. We strictly adhere to Google Search Central's official helpful content guidelines and white-hat outreach standards. By focusing on real human search intent and genuine publisher relationships, our client websites consistently survive and gain ground during core algorithm refreshes.

We accept international wire transfers, major credit/debit cards via Stripe/PayPal, Payoneer, Wise, and direct bank transfers. Custom invoicing is provided for all enterprise orders.
